MPC-HC keyboard shortcuts in MPC-RV

MPC-RV, the free MPC-HC-style video player for Linux, ships with MPC-HC's default shortcuts. These are all 90 of them, plus the mouse and media keys. Every one can be changed in Options › Keys, and the player shows them in its menus too.
